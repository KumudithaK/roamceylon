# Journey Studio essentials fix

## Outcome

Journey designers can now revise arrival and departure dates plus adult, child and infant counts directly in Journey Studio.

## Safeguards

- The original Traveller Brief remains read-only and unchanged.
- Journey Studio changes are saved only to the curated itinerary and recorded in its change history.
- Pickup and drop-off dates remain synchronized with the revised journey dates.
- Reducing traveller totals caps experience participant counts to the corresponding trip totals.
- Date or traveller-count revisions mark existing supplier allocations for review before a revised proposal is prepared.
- Invalid dates or participant totals prevent the journey from proceeding to allocation.

## Database impact

No schema or migration change was required.
