## Hambantota correction basis

The original record had a short sporting summary, a stadium hero, two published
sporting Experiences, and no full or editorial destination copy. CR3 fills only
that presentation gap. The factual basis was checked against Sri Lanka Tourism's
Hambantota and Bundala material and the Ramsar Sites Information Service entry
for Bundala. No opening hours, fees, travel times, availability, or supplier
commitments are introduced.

Sources consulted:

- https://www.srilanka.travel/ArrivebyaCruise/hambanthota-port.php
- https://srilanka.travel/index.php?destination=9&route=attractions%2Fdestination
- https://srilanka.travel/attraction?attraction_id=168
- https://rsis.ramsar.org/sites/default/files/rsiswp_search/exports/Ramsar-Sites-annotated-summary-Sri-Lanka.pdf
