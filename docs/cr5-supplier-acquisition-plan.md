# CR5 founder supplier acquisition plan

This plan describes capabilities to acquire; it intentionally names no invented
supplier. The founder should begin with a narrow route set and expand only when
the corresponding supply chain is verified.

## Minimum information by supplier class

| Class | Required before use | Required before payment | Useful / optional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accommodation | Legal/trading identity; authorised contact; property/location; room and occupancy scope; meal basis; current net rates and validity; taxes/service-charge treatment; child/extra-bed policy; cancellation/no-show; availability/blackout process; emergency contact; evidence supporting any category/credential claim | Invoice identity; agreed currency and payment timing; remittance/bank details collected through an approved private process; tax documentation where applicable; booking/reference and cancellation reconciliation | Sustainability practices, driver/guide lodging, accessibility detail, richer media, preferred amenities |
| Transport | Legal/trading identity; dispatcher/emergency contact; service area; verified vehicle categories and true passenger/luggage limits; driver model; rates/validity; fuel/toll/parking/overtime treatment; cancellation; registration, licence and insurance evidence where applicable; safety/maintenance process | Invoice identity; agreed currency/payment terms; private remittance details; trip record and extras reconciliation | Fleet photos, Wi-Fi/child-seat capability, sustainability detail, named backup fleet |
| Guide | Identity/contact; guide type and genuine geographic/Experience scope; languages; current credential evidence where applicable; daily/half-day/overtime/overnight rates; cancellation; availability method; emergency contact; code of conduct | Invoice identity; agreed payment terms/currency; private remittance details; service-date reconciliation | Biography/media, association membership, specialist portfolio, additional languages |
| Experience provider | Legal/trading identity; authorised contact; exact service/inclusions; location and access rights; operating schedule/season; capacity and participant limits; safety/emergency procedure; equipment; rates/validity/cancellation; licence/permit/insurance/credential evidence where applicable; responsible-tourism policy | Invoice identity; agreed currency/payment terms; private remittance details; attendance/service record and cancellation reconciliation | Exclusive access, richer media, sustainability reporting, value-adds |

Bank, tax, licence, permit and insurance requirements vary by supplier and
activity. CR5 makes no claim about Sri Lankan legal sufficiency: **LEGAL
VERIFICATION REQUIRED** before adopting a mandatory compliance checklist. Never
place bank details in public application data or general internal notes.

## P0 — required for controlled launch

### Accommodation

- Contract primary and backup options for Colombo, the Cultural Triangle base,
  Kandy, one tea-country route, one southern base and Yala.
- Aim for at least two usable stay categories at each sold overnight base, with
  a primary and fallback property. A route without verified coverage is not sold.
- Confirm room/occupancy, meals, driver/guide logistics, full tax/service-charge
  treatment, cancellation and rate validity before quoting.

### Transport

- One reliable island-capable chauffeur operator with an appropriately sized
  private car/SUV and van capability, plus an independently callable backup.
- Confirm real passenger/luggage capacity, airport transfer, day-hire/per-km or
  journey rates, driver costs, overtime, toll/parking/fuel treatment, emergency
  escalation and evidence appropriate to legal operation.

### Guides

- One verified primary national/chauffeur-guide capability for the launch route,
  plus a backup.
- Verified local/specialist access for Cultural Triangle, Kandy, Galle and the
  wildlife/adventure services actually offered. Create Experience links only
  after competence is evidenced.

### Experience providers

- Contract only the Experience set included in the first controlled itineraries:
  Cultural Triangle heritage access, one Kandy cultural service, one Galle
  service, one responsible safari chain, and any selected hike/marine/adventure
  activity.
- Each safety-sensitive service needs a named operator, capacity, operating
  conditions, emergency procedure, applicable credentials/insurance and a
  backup or an honest removal/substitution rule.
- Do not sell any of the 16 QUESTIONABLE or seven held Experiences until its
  specific evidence matrix is satisfied and a human changes its decision.

## P1 — expand after the first routes are reliable

- Build east-coast seasonal coverage (Arugam Bay, Pasikudah, Trincomalee), north
  (Jaffna/Wilpattu), Kalpitiya, Anuradhapura/Polonnaruwa and deeper south-coast
  choice (Bentota, Ahungalla, Weligama, Tangalle, Mirissa).
- Add verified boutique/value/luxury alternatives and accessible options at the
  most requested bases.
- Add regional transport backup, mini-coach capability for larger parties, and
  specialist guides for marine, wildlife, adventure, wellness and cultural
  programmes.
- Resolve the QUESTIONABLE catalogue according to observed demand rather than
  onboarding every possible provider in advance.

## P2 — network depth

- Secondary stays for en-route or excursion destinations, specialist language
  guides, niche vehicles, high-capacity groups and replacement providers.
- Optional privileges and preferred commercial terms only after the basic
  service chain, reconciliation and traveller support work reliably.
- Reconsider held Experiences only when new evidence creates a genuinely safe,
  distinct and ethical proposition.

## Minimum controlled-launch network

A practical founder-led starting network is:

- six route bases: Colombo, Cultural Triangle, Kandy, tea country, south coast
  and Yala;
- primary plus backup accommodation at every sold base, across at least two stay
  categories where the local market makes that feasible;
- one primary chauffeur fleet and one independent backup able to cover the whole
  accepted route and party size;
- one primary guide and one backup, plus named local/specialist capability only
  where the itinerary actually needs it;
- one confirmed primary provider and a fallback/substitution rule for every
  safety-, access- or schedule-dependent Experience in the accepted proposal.

This is deliberately not a nationwide launch. Journey acceptance is constrained
to the verified network. The network can grow with real enquiries and operating
evidence.

## Founder workflow

1. Choose the initial sellable route family and freeze unsupported destinations
   and Experiences from proposals, without deleting catalogue content.
2. Approach capabilities in P0 order; do not submit applications for businesses.
3. Ask suppliers to submit or provide evidence through an approved private
   channel. Record provenance and reviewer.
4. Review applications, request missing evidence, and approve only after the
   commercial checklist is satisfied.
5. Convert approved applications to inactive draft catalogue rows; verify,
   enrich, add coverage and rates, then independently publish when appropriate.
6. Run a dry itinerary with real dates, traveller counts, route, supplier costs,
   cancellation rules and backups before accepting a paid journey.
7. Revalidate rates and evidence on an explicit calendar; suspend use when
   evidence, rates or operating conditions expire.

## CR6 boundary

CR6 owns markup, margins, selling price, deposits, payment schedules and finance
controls. CR5 supplies only verified supplier net inputs, validity, quantities,
terms and evidence. No fictional rate or commercial promise was created here.
