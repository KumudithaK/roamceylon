# CR2 Batch 2 — Core public editorial surfaces

## Visual architecture

Batch 2 evolves the Coming Soon art direction into a functional public travel experience: full-bleed photography, high-contrast Forest anchors, editorial serif scale, fine Gold rules, asymmetric image rhythm and quieter border-led information structures. The homepage now reads as one journey through point of view, Editions, places, experiences, map context, brand approach and journey planning.

Shared catalogue and media-card components establish the repeatable image-led language for Editions and destinations. Destination and Edition details use numbered editorial sequences, image mosaics and border-led related content instead of repeated boxed cards. Experiences retain their tested interactive discovery and participant contracts while gaining a cinematic opening. Our Story already met the intended editorial standard and was retained.

## Public surfaces

- Homepage: fully re-composed cinematic/editorial narrative.
- Editions and Discover: cinematic catalogue plus richer image-led Edition detail.
- Destinations: cinematic catalogue and editorial detail/story/related modules.
- Experiences: cinematic catalogue opening; established editorial detail retained.
- Our Story: existing truthful cinematic composition retained.
- Contact: image-led opening, verified contacts and quieter editorial form composition.
- Partners: cinematic industry-specific opening; existing application contract retained.
- The Ceylon Journal: truthful, deliberately quiet empty state; no fabricated articles.

## Responsive, accessibility and performance

Layouts collapse intentionally from twelve-column editorial compositions to readable two-column and single-column flows. Semantic headings, labelled forms, keyboard focus, 44px controls and reduced-motion behaviour remain. Published catalogue imagery is rendered through Next Image with responsive sizes; only genuine opening imagery is priority-loaded, with below-fold images left lazy by default. No new client or animation dependency was introduced.

## Safety and compatibility

All customer-facing facts come from existing code or published catalogue data. No customer records or staging content are created. Journey Builder and Admin remain outside Batch 2. Database schema, API/query contracts, Storage architecture, RCJ references, internal theme naming and all legacy `roam-ceylon-*` persistence/handoff keys remain unchanged.

## Deferred

The compact-wordmark sub-pixel polish item remains deferred for CR2 final visual polish. Human cinematic visual acceptance remains mandatory after staging deployment.
